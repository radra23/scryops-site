The blocky chickens scattered through the guides are the *field sprites* — the party as you meet them mid-quest. This is the roster at full portrait resolution: the adventurers who keep the service alive, each sworn to a different signal, each with a grievance about being paged.

Every class maps to a real discipline. The Monk watches uptime; the Paladin guards the error budget; the Sorcerer *is* the unknown-unknown; the Warlock answers the pager; the Artificer builds the pipeline that carries it all. New recruits arrive as the quest demands them.
