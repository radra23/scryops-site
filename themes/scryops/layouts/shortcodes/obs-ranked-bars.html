{{- /* layouts/shortcodes/obs-ranked-bars.html · TEMPLATE 01 Ranked quantities, one variable, ordered categories. Bars step DOWN the sequential value ramp in rank order. Bar length is the real signal; the printed figure is the redundant third channel; a 1px ink outline keeps the faintest step edged. No legend — categories are named at the axis, which starts at zero. Ramp steps are assigned BY RANK, computed here, so rows may be listed in category order and the brightest step still lands on the largest. `emphasis` singles out one row WITHOUT colour: outline 1px -> 3.5px and the label to full ink at weight 600. Never tint a bar. {{< obs-ranked-bars title="Support volume by channel" units="tickets, last 30 days" emphasis="0" >}} [ {"label":"Email","value":4200}, {"label":"In-app","value":3100}, {"label":"Phone","value":2450} ] {{< /obs-ranked-bars >}} */ -}} {{- $rows := slice -}} {{- with trim .Inner " \n\r\t" }}{{ $rows = . | transform.Unmarshal }}{{ end -}} {{- $n := len $rows -}} {{- $emph := cond (ne (.Get "emphasis") nil) (int (.Get "emphasis" | default "-1")) -1 -}} {{- $unit := .Get "unit" | default "" -}} {{- /* scale maximum */ -}} {{- $hi := 0.0 -}} {{- range $rows }}{{ $v := index .
